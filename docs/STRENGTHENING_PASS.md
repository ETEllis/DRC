## Material changes

- Separate the developmental mechanism hypothesis from the phenotype–environment hypothesis. Neither theory proves the other.
- Distinguish AVP/copeptin evidence from OXT: Fill Malfertheiner et al. (2021) is direct counterevidence to a uniform birth-hormone response claim.
- Retain the striking Wellmann copeptin contrast only with assay, compartment and cohort scope. Do not infer persistence.
- Replace vaginal-only seeding with maternal reservoirs, feeding and ongoing ecology.
- Drop any universal “90–95% lost L. reuteri” claim; a harmonized prevalence synthesis was not performed.
- Add Mazzone et al. (2024): positive social findings with null overall autism-severity results. Keep SB-121 eye-tracking and OXT non-significance visible.
- Treat α₂δ-1 synaptogenesis and α₂δ-dependent release as separate mechanisms. Do not generalize all isoforms or equate postsynaptic Rac1 biology with channel trafficking.
- Bound channel reallocation to rat auditory synapses; remove a universal human developmental timetable and unsupported hypoxia-specific splice claims.
- Separate specific CAH-X human genetics from the broader, unproven RCCX cluster and the absent direct RCCX → α₂δ bridge.
- Operationalize RCB with positive commensurate gains, repeated measurement and independent validation; do not hide zero/negative estimates with an arbitrary epsilon.
- Treat Dąbrowski bifurcation as an interpretation to operationalize, not an established biological stage or moral ranking.
- Preserve adult pharmacological response only as hypothesis-generation provenance. No personal medication history or dose is published.
- Include adjusted NHS II null estimates; dimensional endpoints must be preregistered to avoid moving goalposts.
- Preserve VMT's positive social-emotional and null global developmental contrasts, separate randomized and natural-reference groups, and reject automatic causal inference from engraftment subgroups.
- Replace same-time cycles with time-indexed feedback.
- Reduce the program to three core discriminators: exposure/mediator intervention, prospective cohort, and randomized regulatory-environment study; a neonatal intervention is contingent.

## Search scope and limitations

Targeted queries covered molecular development, birth signaling, transmission, L. reuteri human trials, null birth-mode findings, behavioral immune claims, RCCX genetics, stress embedding and AI-assisted output. Primary publications were preferred; the Howard meta-analysis is an explicit supplied literature anchor. This pass is not a systematic review or meta-analysis. No PRISMA completeness, risk-of-bias adjudication or raw-data reanalysis is claimed.

The full prior project chat archive and all possible associated notes were not retrieved. The current explicit directive and three named source documents define the implemented corpus. A literature audit can expand the ledger without silently changing the source papers.
