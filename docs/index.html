04 — MAKE IT FAILABLE

Three discriminating studies.

Each study names a result that would favor a simpler rival explanation.

PROTOCOL 01 DEVELOPMENT

Find the perinatal mediator.

Pair a longitudinal birth cohort with stage-specific cellular perturbation. Measure exposure, isoform, tissue, genotype, and later outcomes separately.

Against the theory

The proposed mediator is absent at the predicted stage, or exposure effects vanish under family and cell-composition controls.

PROTOCOL 02 REGULATION

Run the task cross-over.

Randomize feedback density, event rate, movement, delay, and external representations within participants. Estimate regulatory gain across task families.

Against the theory

The proposed orientation has no stable rank or predictive interaction beyond task preference, arousal, and diagnosis.

PROTOCOL 03 CONVERSION

Separate output from novelty.

Randomize AI and scaffold access in mixed-skill teams. Blind-score technical quality, originality, integration, and durable uptake.

Against the theory

AI effects are generic, with no integration-skill interaction, or output grows while collective novelty contracts.
